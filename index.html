Three takeaways

01A cliff, not a slopeIsoScore climbs to a peak at layer L, then falls sharply at L+1. ID instead shows a gradual hump.

What is isotropy?

Isotropy measures how evenly variance is spread over directions. IsoScore is 1 for a perfectly round cloud and near 0 when variance collapses onto a few directions.

Drag to concentrate the variance. A drop in isotropy at the cliff means the model sacrifices uniformity to compress information into a task-relevant, lower-dimensional manifold.

Why it matters

  
The cliff marks where the model becomes decisive. Just before it, representations are most evenly spread, which may give more directional freedom for activation steering. These layers are also natural candidates for fine-tuning or freezing.

  
Limitations
    
We analyze adjacent layer pairs only; some models (Qwen2.5-3B, Mistral-7B-v0.3) show several shifts. Evaluation is limited to multiple-choice QA and five model families. Open-ended generation is future work.
